# Панорама кабины

Создана встроенным imagegen по предоставленному пользователем референсу кабины. Это широкий непрозрачный рисунок для первого теста осмотра в 2D. Перспектива и освещение нарисованы в самой картинке. Дополнительные слои для езды пока не выделены.

Исходный запрос:

Create ONE extremely wide horizontal panoramic 2D game background, aspect ratio 4:1, ideally 4096x1024, depicting about 180 degrees of view from ONE fixed seated tram driver's eye position. Use the supplied picture as the exact artistic reference: scruffy hand-painted semi pixel art, dark charcoal olive woods and rusty ochre tram interior, rough stepped brush marks, cinematic subdued eerie autumn atmosphere, absolutely 2D illustration. This is a continuous panoramic drawing that a 1280x720 game camera will pan across, NOT three separate pictures and NOT a contact sheet. The MIDDLE ONE THIRD must show a complete straight-ahead view of the original tram cab: broad front windshield containing the forest track curving into fog, amber grasses, small stop shelter, entire low worn ochre dashboard with analog speedometer and controller lever along the bottom, rear-view mirror near upper right. Everything important in that central view should fit vertically. Extend naturally to the FAR LEFT with the driver's left-side tram window, weathered window latch, forest beside the tram and the edge of a worn green driver's coat hanging on the side wall. Extend naturally to the FAR RIGHT with the wood and rusty metal cab partition, old radio handset, narrow partly open door and a glimpse of the dim passenger aisle with empty wooden seats. Make the central front view wide enough that when camera is centered it sees forward, and panning reveals the side regions. Panoramic perspective can be gently curved to approximate looking around from one fixed seat. Keep identical rail age, lighting, materials, no characters, no faces, no UI, no lettering, no borders or panel dividers. Fully opaque artwork, no transparent areas. No 3D rendering, no modern clean vector lines, no fisheye black corners. One cohesive wide background suitable for learning 2D camera panning.
