## interior

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 2D game background for the same moody rough hand-painted semi-pixel Russian autumn horror game, widescreen 16:9 1792x1024. First person seated inside an old suburban passenger ELECTRIC TRAIN, looking sideways at a VERY LARGE WINDOW which dominates left and center. Not a driver's cab, no steering controls. The window has one single broad uninterrupted pane, almost axis-aligned, glass interior roughly from 6% to 72% of image width and 10% to 70% of image height. Crucial: entire inner glass area is filled with one FLAT SOLID MUTED SAGE GREEN, no scenery, no reflections or streaks INSIDE the glass, no window mullions crossing the glass. This is a compositing slot, scenery will be animated there. All around: worn scratched dark olive-painted metal frame, faded ochre wall, small luggage shelf above, warm old lamp at upper right, plush brown bench at right, empty coat hook, slightly crooked route placard without readable text. Lower right a small wooden table with a folded paper ticket, thermos and simple cloth travel bag; seated protagonist dark trouser knees bottom foreground, no hands, no people, no face, no text or UI. Painterly broken ink edges and chunky uneven pixelated brushwork, deep desaturated green, umber and muted mustard, nostalgic and faintly unsettling, softly lit warm interior. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not clean smooth illustration. Keep the big blank glass clean and its rectangular corners clearly visible for a moving-background mask. Full opaque background.
