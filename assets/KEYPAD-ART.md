Below screen at y=940..1100 are two blank rectangular soft keys on left and right and a center directional navigation pad with four directions around a round blank OK button, plus small blank green/red call keys. ALL button caps must be BLANK: no numerals and no lettering, since the game will overlay functional labels. Give enough surface on each cap for a number and tiny letters. Screen must be broad and high enough for reading a dialogue, and keypad must be clearly recognizable. Keep the screen and all rows axis aligned to make interactive overlays possible. Game aesthetic: worn Russian apartment at autumn sunset, dark earthy greens and amber, hand-painted gritty pixels. Transparent alpha all around phone, no ground shadow beyond object.
